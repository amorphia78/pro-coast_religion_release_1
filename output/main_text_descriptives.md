# Main-text numbers: descriptives

Generated by src/religion_descriptives.R: every number it produces for the main text that is in no output table, plus the Participants passages, which restate some Table S3 counts as prose.

## Sample sizes

- Full sample: 821 respondents. Of these, 719 are from the 8 case studies that contribute to the models; the remaining 102 are from a further case study whose questionnaire did not include the religiosity item (F7), so none of them can enter any model.
- Primary sample (Model 3, ideology as anti-diversity): 569
- Model 1: 590
- Model 3, ideology as left-right: 501

## Participants (primary sample, n = 569)

### Gender

Gender was reported by all 569 respondents in this sample, because it is a model covariate and the models are complete-case. Before the pre-registered collapse to non-man versus man, the sample comprised 351 women (61.7%), 213 men (37.4%), 3 another gender (0.5%), and 2 non-binary (0.4%).

### Education

Education was likewise reported by all 569 respondents, as it is also a model covariate. Of these, 338 (59.4%) held a university degree, that is, a bachelor's degree or equivalent or higher.

### Religious denomination

Religious denomination was reported by 546 respondents (23 did not answer): 229 no denomination (41.9%), 129 Catholic (23.6%), 77 Protestant (14.1%), 68 Orthodox (12.5%), 20 Free Church (3.7%), 16 another denomination (2.9%), 2 Hindu (0.4%), 2 Buddhist (0.4%), 2 Islam (0.4%), and 1 Jewish (0.2%).

### Stakeholder category

Stakeholder category, grouped from each respondent's role in the case study area, was known for 568 respondents (1 could not be categorised): 396 local community members (69.7%), 107 tourists (18.8%), 26 commercial stakeholders (4.6%), 21 professionals and protectors (3.7%), 11 government stakeholders (1.9%), and 7 wild resource users (1.2%).

## Measures

- CPEB (A26, A27, A28), Cronbach's α: 0.77 in the primary sample (n = 569); 0.77 across all respondents
- Social conservatism items F9 and F16 (both reverse-coded): r = 0.258 across the 752 respondents who answered both
